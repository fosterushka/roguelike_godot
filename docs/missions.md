# 120 missions

Open **Hideout → Tasks** before a raid. Accept up to five missions. Use search, category and status filters to browse the board; the All filter also shows missions locked by account level. During a raid, press **I / Tab → Tasks** to read the journal. The HUD shows progress for the first three active missions.

Career missions accumulate progress across successful raids after acceptance. Raid missions require all objectives and conditions in one successful run. Success means extraction or final victory; objectives that explicitly require an extraction or E1/E2/E3 still require leaving through a zone. Death and abandoning a raid lose unbanked progress. Pauses and hit stop do not add mission time or distance.

After returning, claim rewards on the board. Delivery missions also consume the listed items from the vault. Bought or packed gear does not count as newly looted cargo. Only newly collected items still carried when the raid ends contribute to loot objectives. Missions pay once; abandoning an active mission frees its slot and resets its progress. Existing progress for First delivery, Road keeper and Helping hand is preserved.

Find scrap and electronics in salvage pickups, repair kits in settlement rescue crates, weapon kits in convoy/scavenger crates, and relics in foundry crates. Fuel cans come from the trader. Capacity starts at 12 slots and can be upgraded to 24; relics and weapon kits each occupy two slots.

There are 12 missions in each of ten categories. Account levels 1–8 unlock progressively harder tasks. The catalogue combines enemy types, driving, weapons, activities, weather, loot and extraction conditions; these are contracts using the existing battlefield systems.

## Implementation

Content: `data/missions.json`. Validated cached catalog: `modules/meta/mission_catalog.gd`. Event and telemetry tracking: `mission_tracker.gd`; multi-objective progress and save migration: `mission_progress.gd`; economy transactions: `expedition.gd`. The board creates only the current page of mission controls, and the HUD polls at most five active missions twice a second.

Automated coverage: `mission_catalog_test.gd`, `mission_tracker_test.gd`, `mission_board_test.gd`, `mission_flow_test.gd`, and the new mission assertions in `extraction_zone_flow_test.gd`. These check data consistency, progress rules, rollback/retry, old saves, mission limits, filters, gameplay events and real zone extraction. They do not establish difficulty balance for a full playthrough of every mission.

## Validation

63/63 automated suites pass across the full regression run and final targeted checks. The graphical capture completed 11 frames in Russian and English at 1280×800 and 960×600, using the actual Main scene and an isolated test profile. The narrow mission HUD was resized after visual inspection to clear the central event banner. Existing compiler warnings remain; there were no script or engine errors in the capture.
