## Ground effect depth correction

Scorch and blood quads previously used `rotation = (-PI/2, random, 0)` with XYZ Euler order. This rotated the surface normal into the scene and let portions of a large mark cross enemies and pickup geometry. They now use source local-Z rotation `(-PI/2, 0, random)`, leaving every vertex at the ground height. Scorches retain depth testing, do not write alpha depth and receive a bounded, chronological negative render priority, so camera shake cannot reorder overlapping marks and airborne transparency renders after ground marks. Pool rollover replaces only its oldest scorch; transient material reuse resets ground-specific draw state. Fireballs no longer use globally elevated priorities 18/19 over other transparent objects; their core uses a small local sorting offset, while smoke keeps ordinary scene depth sorting.

`tests/ground_effect_depth_test.gd` passes 297 checks for all quad corner heights across 24 randomized marks, collision retention and texture identity, stable ordering through rollover, material reuse and airborne depth policy. These scene-state checks reproduce and prevent the tilted geometry bug; actual GPU occlusion and alpha blending still need graphical validation. Godot priority and sort behavior follow [Material.render_priority](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_material.html#class-material-property-render-priority) and [VisualInstance3D.sorting_offset](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_visualinstance3d.html#class-visualinstance3d-property-sorting-offset).

## Airborne mud and terrain contact correction

The later screenshot exposed an additional path: world explosion events passed their impact altitude into `WeatherState.create_mud`, and `WeatherView` added only 0.017 to that height. A rocket striking at y=3 therefore produced a 10x10 mud plane through the vehicle at y=3.017. Both domain creation and presentation now resolve the terrain height from X/Z, independently of impact altitude. Mud textures are selected by persistent zone ID instead of pool slot, so removing an earlier zone no longer changes surviving textures. Restart invalidates cached zone geometry.

`GroundSurfaceView` clips the actual shared terrain triangles to each mud/scorch/blood texture footprint, preserving UVs and matching terrain height throughout each triangle, not only at its center. Small instanced tire marks follow ground height and normal. Render layering is explicit: roads, tracks, mud, retained blood/scorches, then airborne transparent visuals; ground meshes still test opaque actor/building depth. Surface offsets are 0.018/0.020/0.024/0.028m for tracks/mud/scorch/blood, avoiding the road-junction height of 0.014m. Real fireball and smoke positions retain their airborne impact height.

`tests/ground_surface_test.gd` passes 1034 checks, including high/negative impact heights, stale elevated state records, every generated vertex and triangle center against the terrain heightfield, stable mud texture identity after expiry, restart reuse and real airborne effects. Ground-depth tests remain 297/297; source VFX 1467/1467, presentation details35/35 and world gameplay41/41 pass. Actual GPU rendering was not attempted in this pass.

Ground conformity regression also compares every emitted face winding to the native upward PlaneMesh, so default back-face culling cannot hide mud. Player wreck fall preserves the original chassis height; retained enemy wrecks sample the terrain, and death-material warmup uses the wheeled rig.
